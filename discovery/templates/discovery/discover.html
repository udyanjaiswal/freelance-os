How It Works

Freelancer OS gathers live business listings and automatically identifies the best prospects for your freelance services.

1. Location Gathering
Extracts businesses with names, addresses, phones, and websites.
2. Automatic Deduplication
Prevents duplicate entries by matching existing phone numbers and addresses.
3. Opportunity Scoring
High scores are awarded to businesses missing a website or with incomplete digital presence.
Scoring Breakdown:
• +25 pts: No website listed (ideal web design prospect)
• +15 pts: Map or directory profile only
• +10 pts: Verified phone number for outreach
